Classroom Arena

Classroom Arena is a fast-paced third-person action combat game set in a gauntlet-style game mode where players must survive through continuous waves of enemies using combo-based melee combat, offensive abilities, and powerful ultimate skills. The gameplay emphasises aggression, timing, and combat mastery as players overcome increasingly intense enemy encounters.

Attacks

Normal Attack

A four-hit combo attack sequence that serves as the core combat mechanic, allowing players to chain attacks together through timing-based inputs for continuous offensive pressure

Ability

A cooldown-based single-target attack that performs a rapid multi-phase strike sequence designed to deliver high damage and enhance one-on-one combat effectiveness

Ultimate

A high-damage area-of-effect attack that delivers a powerful and visually impactful finishing strike against multiple enemies
