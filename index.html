About Me

Hi, I'm Jeffrey — a Game Design postgraduate from Escape Studios and a Computer Science graduate from the University of Birmingham.

I have a strong passion for game development, particularly the relationship between gameplay mechanics, player decision-making, and engaging combat experiences.

My primary focus is designing and implementing gameplay systems from the ground up, including combat mechanics, enemy AI, progression systems, and interactive content.

I enjoy exploring how technical implementation and design principles can work together to create meaningful and entertaining player experiences.
